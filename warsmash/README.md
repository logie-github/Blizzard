# Embedded Warsmash

Blizzard embeds sandstranger's Android Warsmash 1.0.4 (F-Droid version code 5), not a second installed app. `scripts/prepare-warsmash.sh` fetches the published source archive and verifies SHA-256 `95845f526c08a106d16a3cb9d083dbb8758fe052130dc9f160201d30c0c461ac`. Sources, the upstream license and bundled Android backends go into ignored `app/warsmash/`. No Warcraft game files are committed or bundled.

Source: https://f-droid.org/repo/net.warsmash.phone_5_src.tar.gz
Upstream: https://github.com/Retera/WarsmashModEngine
Android port: sandstranger, https://f-droid.org/en/packages/net.warsmash.phone/
License: GNU Affero General Public License v3.0; preserve upstream and dependency notices. The full upstream archive remains available from the source URL; the build script records the local adaptation (explicit archive ordering instead of the directory scan).

The library modules compile the core, parsers, shared code, AWT compatibility code and native pathfinding. The host supplies the game folder, an explicitly ordered data-source configuration and the game to play: the engine's GameVersion, 0 for Reign of Chaos and 1 for The Frozen Throne (both of Warcraft III's tiles play here). The launcher's changes to the engine are patches in `patches/warsmash/`, applied in order by the build script after the upstream copy (`0001`: a finger on the minimap pans the view as dragging the battlefield would, tracked by its own pointer so it never clicks, selects or counts toward a double click and the other fingers play on; `0002`: no frame counter over the game or its menus; `0003`: Warcraft III's movies through `MovieHost` (the host's player, which the activity sets: the introduction at the first start, each campaign's opening at its first entry and its movies in its mission list, PlayCinematic's movie when the game ends) and the game's end itself (EndGame and ChangeLevel, dialog buttons that return their handles, DialogAddQuitButton); `0004`: folder data sources find a file in any case (the game names its files as Windows lets it), and the BLP2 reader works (header at its byte offsets, the whole palette, unsigned DXT colours): for Classic+, the app's optional model and texture pack layered over the game; `0006`: Move aimed at an item the unit can carry issues the inventory's getitem order (it walks over and picks it up); `0005`: a unit whose data asks for AlternateEx animations (the Prophet's raven, the druids' and demon hunters' forms) plays the model's Alternate ones where it has no AlternateEx sequence, as the original does; `0007`: the Far Seer's Far Sight (AOfs: a timed circle of vision for the caster's player at the point) and Earthquake (AOeq: channeled; after Data A seconds, Data B damage per second to buildings and Data C slow on ground units, in an area growing from the ability's area to Data D); `0008`: 58 abilities from Warsmash Reforged (nikoltu/W3AndroidEngine, AGPL-3.0, a later fork of the same engine), their classes copied and adapted to this engine's ability interface (no player index or auto-order argument), registered in `CAbilityData`, with what they need added beside the engine's own code: `CDamageFlags` and its kinds onto the engine's damage call, buff and ability kinds (magic, positive, dispellable), healing factor, area targeting, Ultravision, timer step helpers, Frost slows, a timed buff's carried time, Rain of Fire as a Blizzard with its own order and damage; and two fixes of the engine's own: "ancient,nonancient" targets allow both, and a unit's tick survives an ability removing more than itself; `warsmash/rig` runs them on a desktop; `0009`: 11 more of Reforged's (Death and Decay, Starfall, Mana Flare, Phase Shift, Big Bad Voodoo, Unsummon, Devour, Flare and the reveals) with `CPausedTickingBuff`, `CHarmfulAreas` (recorded only), the spell-effect listener and the `NOAUTOCASTBEHAVIOR` autocast kind; and the Java spells' behaviors fire the spell events (channel, cast, effect, finish, end-cast) and end a no-target channel through the ability; `0010`: corpses (`becomeCorpse`, held and released by a Meat Wagon), illusions (`makeIllusion`: damage dealt and taken factors, no food or hero place, spells removed, `IsUnitIllusion`), `changeOwner` (food, hero list, tech tree, upgrades; `SetUnitOwner` uses it), hero copy and retrain, and 14 of Reforged's abilities on them; `0011`: Reforged's ability builder and its 197 JSON abilities (`core/assets/abilityBehaviors`, now read through `Gdx.files.internal` from the app's assets, where the build script copies it; `INHERIT` entries joined to their `parentId` before registration), the attack system (`CUnitAttackSettings`, replacements, modifiers and their priority loop, `CDamageFlags` through every damage call, `EVENT_UNIT_ATTACKED`), the unit-state buffs (polymorph, attack index on/off, move and attack disabled, paused, untargetable, unenumerable) and Holy Light and Storm Bolt registered; `0012`: Locust Swarm, Battle Stations (burrows as a boarding partner, `CAbilityCargoHoldBurrow.canBoard`), Spiked Barricades (the `rspi` upgrade effect sets a per-unit amount), War Club, Pillage, Sentinel, Wander, Spell Book (`containerMenuOrderId` on abilities, shown inside the book's menu by `RenderUnit`), tiny buildings, blight placement, the flags, Decouple and `Apos` registered; and `CBehaviorTargetSpellBase.endMove` ends a channel only when the move was interrupted; `0013`: `GlobalScope.resetGlobalInitialization` forgets `{init-globals}` once run (a `.pld` reran it), `Trigger` actions run at once through `GlobalScope.runThreadNow`, a thread that throws is ended instead of left running, and `NativeJassFunction` turns a native's null-handle failure or missing return into its type's empty value; `0014`: `skills/neutral/alchemist` (Healing Spray, Acid Bomb, Transmute), `tinker` (Engineering Upgrade, Demolish) and `firelord` (Summon Lava Spawn), written for this engine; `0015`: `CAbilityBreathOfFrost` (Breath of Fire's, burning every unit hit) and the Tornado's `seawitch/CAbilityTornadoDamage`, `TornadoSpin`, `TornadoWander`; `0016`: Reforged's `CHarmfulAreas.stepOut`, called from `CUnit.update` for computer players' units, and `CUnit.getLastHurtTick`; `0017`: `CAbilityBlizzard` (also Rain of Fire) registers its area, armed at its second wave; `0018`: `CSimulation.adjustTerrain` and the mod-buffer helpers go through the render controller to `Terrain.adjustGround`, which changes the corners' ground heights and uploads them; `0019`: `TriggerRegisterUnitInRange` (`unit/CUnitInRangeEvent`, updated each tick by `CSimulation`), `GetHeroLevel`, `GetLevelingUnit`, `ShowDestructable`; `0021`: Reforged's AI (`parsers/jass/ai`, `JassAIEnvironment`, `AINatives` with `doAIScript`; the AI program inherits the map's with `GlobalScope.inheritFrom`), run per player from `CSimulation.update`; `CBehaviorHumanRepair` goes on with an unfinished building at full life; Blight reads Data A as the expansion amount; event loops fire over copies and engine-fired triggers are queued (`Trigger`); `-Dwarsmash.computerPlayers` makes slots computers for the rig; `0022`: `CPathfindingProcessor`'s goal-neighbour bounds by rows and columns, a job planned without other units when it starts among them, a 40,000-step limit and a partial path to the nearest explored node; `util/CampaignProgress` with `SetMissionAvailable`, `SetCampaignAvailable`, `SetEdCinematicAvailable`, and `CampaignMenuUI.showWhen`/`refresh`); `0023`: `AIHarvest` gives wood jobs to gatherers that aren't workers (ghouls) when they follow, move or stand, and to the attack group's while it is home, sends a stopped one back to its job, and orders a loaded harvester to return instead of a right-click; `0024`: `CAbilityLandMine` (Amin), creep night sleep in `CUnit` (`canSleep` from UnitData, a `SLEEPING` state, the sleep natives), `Arpb` in the JSON abilities, `CAbilitySellUnits` with `SellStockState` (Reforged's, adapted: the buyer in the order id, as `CAbilitySellItems` does, above a marker bit) from `Sellunits` and the stock fields, the command card's buttons for it, and tech equivalents (`CItemData.getTechEquivalents`, counted by `CPlayer.addTechtreeUnlocked`)); the prepared tree is made again, from a fresh copy, whenever the source or a patch changes. The game runs in `:warsmash`, separate from the launcher and Winlator. Leaving it disposes the screen and ends only that engine process, so another launch starts fresh.

It plays classic MPQ installations (1.22–1.29): war3.mpq, and war3x.mpq for The Frozen Throne. CASC (1.30 and later) and Reforged installations are refused by the importer (`Warcraft3WarsmashImporter`), whose Retry brings Blizzard's 1.27 instead. Android OpenGL ES 3.2 is required by this port's shaders. A keyboard/mouse or gamepad is supported by the upstream engine; the host adds nothing over the game (Back is Escape; the game's menu quits). Device gameplay remains unverified until tested with actual game files.
